Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Spot Solutions Ltd. (Vancouver, BC, Canada)

Spot Solutions is committed to safeguarding personal and proprietary enterprise data in strict accordance with Canada’s PIPEDA (Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act) and British Columbia’s FOIPPA (Freedom of Information and Protection of Privacy Act).

1. Information We Collect

Spot Solutions collects information that you directly provide to us through our website contact forms, consultation requests, or email communications. This information includes your full name, business email address, telephone number, organization/company affiliation, and specific project requirements or technical inquiries.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information solely to assess project scopes, deliver technical proposals, furnish software engineering services, and maintain ongoing commercial communications. Spot Solutions never sells, rents, leases, or trades client contact or commercial data to third parties.

3. Data Protection, Storage & Canadian Residency

We enforce enterprise-grade security protocols across all systems, including parameterized database architecture, cryptographic password hashing, TLS 1.3 encryption for data in transit, and role-based access controls. For public sector and healthcare engagements requiring domestic data residency, all project data is maintained strictly within Canadian Azure or client-specified on-premise data centers.

4. Cookies and Session Tokens

Our web properties utilize strictly necessary HTTP session cookies for user navigation, authentication, and Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF) mitigation. We do not deploy invasive third-party cross-site behavioral tracking cookies.

5. Contact Our Privacy Officer

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, wish to access or rectify your records, or request data deletion under applicable provincial or federal privacy laws, please contact our Privacy Officer:
