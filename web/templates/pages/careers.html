We're less interested in talking about hype than we are in using AI to build reliable software faster. At Spot Solutions, we incorporate Agentic AI deeply into our development lifecycle. You will still need strong fundamental knowledge of architecture, databases, APIs, security, and enterprise testing. The difference is that you will have advanced AI agents helping you design, code, test, and refactor software.

We look for engineers who can comfortably bridge custom software development and Microsoft 365 / SharePoint ecosystems. Microsoft 365 & Cloud Experience
