Everything You Need to Know

Which should I choose: SharePoint Online, Hybrid, or On-Premise?

Online: Minimizes on-premise hardware, delivers automatic feature updates, seamlessly integrates with Copilot and Teams, and keeps operational costs low.
Hybrid: Perfect for organizations retaining critical legacy systems on-premise while leveraging cloud-based collaboration tools.
On-Premises: The right choice for organizations bound by strict data sovereign custody, air-gapped security, or custom database policies.

What are the steps involved in migrating or fixing our SharePoint?

1. Information Architecture Analysis: We inspect site hierarchies, content inventory, permissions, and custom code.
2. Blueprint & Governance Plan: We establish scheduling, metadata tagging structures, security, and milestones.
3. Development & Staging: We configure the target environment and perform trial data transfers.
4. Cutover & Adoption: Smooth final rollout with zero data loss, followed by hands-on user training and SLA support.

My team struggles with SharePoint adoption — how do we make them use it?

Adoption issues happen when systems are forced upon users without solving their everyday pain points. We redesign SharePoint to match the team's daily habits, introduce intuitive navigation, automate tedious paperwork, and conduct tailored, friendly training sessions that demonstrate immediate personal benefit.

What is Change Management?

Organizational change management is critical to the successful implementation of any system, process, or change to a business. Changes in businesses often fail because of a lack of communication. Either it hasn't been accurately communicated to the users, the users refuse to adapt to the changes, or because they feel that they don't understand why change is needed. This is why change management is crucial: it opens the door for communication, and acclimation to change methodically.

This is where Spot Solutions comes in. We have a complete team with experience and expertise in change management certified in leading methodologies. With a communication plan, we'll help you identify what about the changes you need to communicate, when, and who to communicate from. We provide "Ask Me Anything" sessions and pre-change training.

The ADKAR Approach

While there are many change methodologies, the one Spot Solutions has been using most frequently is the Prosci ADKAR approach:

1. Awareness

Identify pain points, create a clear plan, and communicate the issues and planned changes.

2. Desire

Gauge staff reactions, address concerns, and explain personal and company benefits.

3. Knowledge

Provide tailored training and coaching to bridge skills and knowledge gaps.

4. Ability

Ensure required tools are available, conduct practice runs, and monitor initial launch.

5. Reinforcement

Continuous check-ins, recognition, and measurements over time to sustain success.
